Signature Songs

A few standout tracks from each artist, worth a closer listen.

Zach Bryan

Top Favorite

"I Remember Everything" (feat. Kacey Musgraves) — his first Billboard Hot 100 #1, and a 2024 Grammy winner for Best Country Duo/Group Performance.

"Something in the Orange" — one of his most iconic tracks, past a billion streams on Spotify.

"Revival" — the closing track from Elisabeth that's become his signature concert encore.

Morgan Wallen

In the Rotation

"Last Night" — his first #1 on the Billboard Hot 100, from One Thing at a Time (2023).

"Whiskey Glasses" — the breakout single that put him on the map, from If I Know Me (2018).

"Sand In My Boots" — a Country Airplay #1 from Dangerous: The Double Album.

Toby Keith

The Legend

"Should've Been a Cowboy" — his 1993 debut single, later named the most-played country song of the 1990s.

"Red Solo Cup" — the tongue-in-cheek party anthem from Clancy's Tavern (2011).

"Courtesy of the Red, White and Blue" — my all-time favorite song, featured up top.

Cody Johnson

Traditional Roots

"'Til You Can't" — his first Grammy Award (Best Country Song) and first Country radio #1.

"Human" — the title track from Human: The Double Album, a fan favorite.
